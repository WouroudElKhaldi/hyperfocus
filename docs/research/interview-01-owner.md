# Interview 1: the owner (2026-10-04)

The first interview of P0-07: the app's owner and first user, who works as a software engineer and uses every page.
Precise questions, written answers. What we heard, then what it changes in the app (tracked in TASKS.md).

## What we heard

**Daily use**
- Opening the app: wants to see **today's tasks**, and everything else scheduled for today (**events, meetings**), plus
  **a button straight to the Study page**. The Study page must not name a specific course or job (no "software
  engineer", no "SE Square"); its **focus timer is helpful** there too.
- When: no fixed time, mostly **evening or night**. On **both** phone and laptop: the **phone at work** to check quick
  things, the **laptop to learn**.
- Most used: **Study, LinkedIn posts, Emails, Calendar**. Every page is used, at different frequencies; none is
  unnecessary.
- Tasks: **mostly broken into smaller steps**, to be clear about what to do.

**What gets in the way**
- Most crowded today: the **Pet** page ("lots of things going on"). More use needed before naming others.
- **Reminders and notifications help a lot** ("I forget a lot"). Keep them.
- Missed tasks or streaks: **encourage, and state the facts very gently**, never criticise.
- The **pet and XP motivate** strongly ("makes me want to do more to grow my pet"), but **scoring is too easy**: simple
  tasks give as many points as hard ones.

**Look and feel**
- Likes the **purple**, and **changing themes**; uses **light and dark** depending on mood. Wants **two more themes**:
  **all white** and **all black**.
- Text size and spacing: **easy to read** as they are.
- Animations: **keep them** (nice, motivating).
- Menu: wants to **choose what is in it, regroup items and reorder them**.

**Languages**
- Four: **English, French, Arabic (standard), Arabic (Lebanese dialect)**.
- French with **"tu"**.
- **By default the device's language**; changeable from the **navigation bar** and from **Settings**.

**Jobs and skills**
- What makes a posting worth opening: **all of it**: title, company, salary, remote or not, skills.
- Skills: **rates them herself** (would not log enough detail for the app to guess a level).
