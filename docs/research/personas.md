# Proto-personas & journey maps (P0-08)

> ⚠️ **These are assumptions, not research.** They were written before any interviews, to give the team a shared starting
> point. After the interviews in [interview-kit.md](interview-kit.md), mark each assumption ✔ (confirmed), ✘ (wrong) or ~ (partly),
> and rewrite the personas from what students actually said. Until then, don't make big product bets on them.

## Personas

### 1. Lina, Nursing, 3rd year: "I know exactly what to do, I just can't start"
- **Context:** clinical placements and exams, shifting schedule, phone-first.
- **ADHD traits:** task paralysis, time blindness, strong hyperfocus once started.
- **Needs (assumed):** a single "do this next" step; reminders tied to her placement days; hospital/clinic student jobs nearby.
- **Job search (assumed):** looks for paid placements and hospital assistant roles; filters by location and shift type, not by skills.
- **Hyperfocus features she'd use:** Today, I'm stuck, Calendar, reminders, Job Radar with location + schedule filters.

### 2. Karim, Business, 2nd year: "Twenty tabs open, zero applications sent"
- **Context:** wants a summer internship in marketing or finance; unsure which.
- **ADHD traits:** decision fatigue, novelty-seeking, drops tools after a week.
- **Needs (assumed):** a short list instead of endless feeds; help choosing between paths; tracking what he applied to.
- **Job search (assumed):** browses everything, saves lots, applies to almost none. Overwhelmed by choice.
- **Features:** Job Radar "Top 5 today", Jobs pipeline, Email templates, Wins (to see progress).

### 3. Sara, Computer Science, final year: "I hyperfocus on side projects and forget the deadlines"
- **Context:** strong skills, many side projects, applying to junior developer roles.
- **ADHD traits:** hyperfocus, forgets follow-ups, underestimates time.
- **Needs (assumed):** skill-gap view against real job requirements; follow-up reminders; LinkedIn presence.
- **Job search (assumed):** filters by tech stack and remote work; wants skills matched automatically.
- **Features:** Skills map linked to Job Radar skill gaps, LinkedIn posts, Study log, follow-up reminders.

### 4. Omar, Fine Arts, 1st year: "Job boards aren't made for people like me"
- **Context:** freelance and gallery/assistant work; job titles don't match standard lists.
- **ADHD traits:** creative bursts, avoids admin, hates long forms.
- **Needs (assumed):** non-traditional work types (freelance, commissions, volunteer); very short setup; visual, calm UI.
- **Job search (assumed):** relies on Instagram/word of mouth; generic boards return irrelevant results.
- **Features:** Job Radar presets for arts majors, freelance/volunteer types, Notes for ideas, Pet/music for motivation.
- **Risk he shows us:** the taxonomy must cover creative and non-corporate work, or we fail a whole group of students.

## Journey map A: "Find an internship" (assumed)

| Stage | Doing | Thinking / feeling | Pain (assumed) | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Realise it's time | Sees friends applying | "I'm already late" 😰 | Shame, freeze | Kind nudge + tiny first step |
| Decide what to look for | Googles roles for their major | "What can I even do with this degree?" | No idea of options | **Major → occupations presets** (CIP–SOC) |
| Search | Opens 5 job sites | "There's too much" 😵 | Firehose, duplicates | **One feed, deduplicated, Top 5** |
| Choose | Reads listings | "Am I qualified?" | Can't judge fit | **"Why this matches you"** + skill gaps |
| Apply | Writes CV/email | "This takes forever" | Starting the email | Email templates + AI polish (exists) |
| Track & follow up | Forgets | "Did I apply there?" | Lost track, no follow-up | Jobs pipeline + follow-up reminders (exists) |
| Wait / hear back | Checks inbox | Anxiety | Silence | Wins log, pet XP for applying (gentle) |

## Journey map B: "Survive exam week" (assumed)

| Stage | Doing | Feeling | Pain (assumed) | Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| See the exam date | Notes it somewhere | Calm, it's far | Time blindness | Calendar + countdown "in 12 days" |
| Plan | Makes a plan, too ambitious | Motivated | Unrealistic plan | AI "plan my study week" into free slots (exists) |
| Start | Can't start | Guilt | Task paralysis | I'm stuck + 2-minute first step (exists) |
| Study | Hyperfocus or distraction | In the zone / scattered | Forgets breaks or drifts | Focus timer, break nudges, focus music (exist) |
| Last days | Cramming | Panic | Overwhelm | "Top 3 today" only, kind copy |
| After | Relief | Tired | No reflection | Weekly review + wins (exist) |

## Assumptions to check in interviews
1. Students want **fewer, better** job matches (≈5/day), not many. → interview section 3, last job question
2. Students filter by **location, schedule and work type** more than by skills (except CS). → "How did you decide…"
3. Non-CS majors don't know which job titles fit their degree. → "Where did you look? What did you search for?"
4. Tracking applications and following up is a real, common pain. → "How did you keep track…"
5. A daily digest is preferred over instant alerts. → "…when would it feel like too much?"
6. Students would trust matches more if the app explains **why** a job matches. → "What would make you trust…"
